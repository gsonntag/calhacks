Built for the real world

Trust is the product.

  • Approval gate — nothing is said or paid on your behalf without your OK.
  • Never agrees to pay on a call; discloses it's an automated assistant.
  • Audit log of every tool call, with inputs and results.
  • No invented numbers — computed in code, traced to a bill line, EOB field or bank transaction.
  • Verified quotes — every policy number is checked against the hospital's own document.
Feasibility

Why this is real.

  • Bank data — transactions + payroll deposits are available today, with consent, via Plaid / MX / Finicity. Nessie is our sandbox stand-in.
  • Public policies — hospitals must publish them: IRS 501(r); California's Hospital Fair Pricing Act, up to 400% of the poverty line.
  • Warm handoff — the patient verbally authorizes on the call (HIPAA, 45 CFR 164.510(b)).
  • Honest caller — Remy says it's automated and that the call is transcribed.
