Charity Care Policy

Eligible patients can receive "charity care" to have all or part of their hospital bill forgiven. The hospital's charity care policy explains who can get charity care and how that decision is made.

Discount Payment Policy

Eligible patients can receive a discount that reduces their hospital bill to the highest amount expected to be paid by Medicare or Medi-Cal. The hospital's discount payment policy explains who can get discounted payment, how that decision is made, and payment plan options.
