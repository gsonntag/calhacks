## Medicaid changes

The federal government is making changes to Medicaid programs across the country. Please update your contact information and respond to any notices, including the blue renewal form mailed from MassHealth. Please see the below FAQs for resources for Massachusetts, New Hampshire, and Maine residents.

What is happening and when?

Federal health care coverage changes under the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, also referred to as OB3 or OBBBA, may affect some Medicaid members (including MassHealth and Health Connector members) beginning in fall 2026 and continuing into 2027. These changes include:

*   Eligibility changes for some lawfully present immigrants
*   New work and education requirements for some adults
*   More frequent eligibility redeterminations for certain members

Who will be impacted?

Medicaid has indicated that the largest impacts will be for some adults ages 19 to 64 and certain immigrants.

What can I do?

**Members should watch for official notices.** You should read and respond to letters (for MassHealth members this is a blue envelope), emails, texts, or calls.

Make sure your address, phone number, and other contact information is up to date.

Medicaid resources by state of residence:

*   [Massachusetts](https://www.mass.gov/orgs/masshealth)
*   [New Hampshire](https://www.dhhs.nh.gov/programs-services/stay-covered-new-hampshire/stay-covered-nh-information-people-medicaid)
*   [Maine](https://www.maine.gov/dhhs/oms)

What is Mass General Brigham doing?

These changes are going into effect because of federal policy changes to Medicaid programs.

Mass General Brigham is committed to caring for our patients, regardless of insurance or immigration status.

We encourage you to utilize existing resources from your state’s website to answer questions you may have.

Medicaid resources by state of residence:

## State programs

If you live in Massachusetts, you may be able to get health insurance or help paying your health care bills through a state program like [MassHealth](https://www.mass.gov/topics/masshealth), the [Health Connector](https://www.mahealthconnector.org/), or [Health Safety Net](https://www.mass.gov/orgs/health-safety-net). Each program has different rules and income limits.

If you live in New Hampshire, please visit [New Hampshire's state website for coverage options](https://www.dhhs.nh.gov/programs-services/stay-covered-new-hampshire/stay-covered-nh-information-people-medicaid). If you live in Maine, please visit [Maine's state website for coverage options](https://www.maine.gov/dhhs/oms).

## Discounts

If you have medical bills because you do not have insurance, or because your health insurance did not cover your care, you may qualify for a discount.

Find out if you qualify for any of these discounts by referring to the section below on qualification screening for financial assistance.

Read our full Financial Assistance Policy in the section below.

## Payment plans

You may set up a 0% interest monthly payment plan for any bill, even a bill for a health insurance co-payment, co-insurance, or deductible. Log into [Patient Gateway](/en/patients-visitors/resources/becoming-patient/patient-gateway) to set up today or call [617-726-3884](tel:617-726-3884) for details.

Please be aware self-pay estimates for scheduled elective self-pay services are required to be paid at 100% after the allowed 25% discount. Payment plans are for post-service bills.

Take action right away. There may be time limits that apply.

### Contact us to find out more

Please call [617-726-3884](tel:617-726-3884) or e-mail [patientbilling@partners.org](mailto:patientbilling@partners.org) to request information and application forms.

**In person:** Visit a patient financial counselor at your hospital or health center. For a list of patient financial counselors' contact information, please browse to the contact a financial counselor section of this page.

## Qualification screening for financial assistance

State programs

For those with health insurance or need help paying your health care bills through a state program like [MassHealth](https://www.mass.gov/topics/masshealth), the [Health Connector](https://www.mahealthconnector.org/), or [Health Safety Net](https://www.mass.gov/orgs/health-safety-net).

**You may qualify if:**

*   You live in Massachusetts, and
*   You meet income requirements (each program has different rules and income limits)

**How to apply**

*   Visit a patient financial counselor at your hospital or health center (see the contact a financial counselor section for more information).
*   Visit [www.MAhealthconnector.org](https://www.mahealthconnector.org/).

Discounts for low-income patients

70% to 100% discount on bills for an emergency or other medically necessary services, depending on your household income.

**You may qualify if:**

*   You do not qualify for public insurance programs in your home state, and
*   Your bills are for an emergency or other medically necessary services, and
*   Your household income is below 301% of the [Federal Poverty Guidelines](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/program-financial-guidelines-for-certain-masshealth-applicants-and-members), and
*   Your bills are not for insurance co-payments, co-insurance, or deductibles.

**How to apply**

*   Complete and submit a paper application (found in the financial application section) along with ID and income verification.
*   Call [617-726-3884](tel:617-726-3884) or use our Financial Assistance Application (found in the section below).
*   Visit a patient financial counselor at your hospital or health center if you need help (see the contact a financial counselor section for more information).

Discounts for specialty care

60% to 100% discount on bills for care received at Spaulding Rehabilitation Network and McLean Hospital, depending on your household income.

**You may qualify if:**

*   You do not qualify for public insurance programs in your home state, and
*   Your bills are for care received at Spaulding Rehabilitation Network or McLean Hospital, and
*   Your bills are not for insurance co-payments, co-insurance, or deductibles.

**How to apply**

*   Complete and submit a paper application (found in the financial application section) along with ID and income verification.
*   Call [617-726-3884](tel:617-726-3884) or use our Financial Assistance Application (found in the section below).
*   Visit a patient financial counselor at your hospital or health center if you need help (see the contact a financial counselor section for more information).

Discounted payment plans

25% discount on any bills.

**You may qualify if:**

*   Your bills are not for insurance co-payments, co-insurance, or deductibles, and
*   You pay or set up a payment plan within two months of the date on your first bill.

**How to apply**

Call [617-726-3884](tel:617-726-3884) to pay or set up a payment plan.

Payment plans

Monthly payment plan on any bills.

**You may qualify if:**

You have any bill that you are having difficulty paying, even a bill for a health insurance co-payment, co-insurance, or deductible.

**How to apply**

Call [617-726-3884](tel:617-726-3884) to pay or set up a payment plan.
