# Billing and Financial Assistance

## Financial assistance

BJC hospitals want to help patients who do not have health insurance or who need help paying their hospital bills.

To find out which hospitals, health services, doctors and other practitioners offer BJC Financial Assistance, download the following list. Some doctors and providers are affiliated with BJC hospitals, but do not offer Financial Assistance under BJC guidelines. [Download and find providers](/sites/bjc/files/2025-02/BJC-Financial-Assistance-Provider-List.xlsx).

## Patient billing and collection

BJC HealthCare and our member hospitals want to help you understand the billing and collection process and the resources that are available for you and your family.

[Patient Billing & Collection Policy](/sites/bjc/files/2026-03/3259250-FAP-Collection-Policy.pdf) [Patient Billing & Collection Policy — Large Print](/sites/bjc/files/2026-03/3259250-FAP-Collection-Policy-Large.pdf) [American Sign Language Video](https://youtu.be/QLl8U1BL460) [Audio File](/sites/bjc/files/2026-08/3259250-FAP-Collection-Policy.mp3)

## Amounts generally billed

If you qualify for financial assistance, BJC hospitals cannot charge you more than the amounts generally billed to individuals with health insurance. Learn more about how amounts generally billed are calculated to ensure you are not being charged more than a patient with insurance coverage.

[Amounts Generally Billed](/sites/bjc/files/2026-03/Amounts-Generally-Billed.pdf) [Amounts Generally Billed — Large Print](/sites/bjc/files/2026-03/Amounts-Generally-Billed-Large.pdf) [American Sign Language Video](https://youtu.be/V9DhY_9VNEU) [Audio File](/sites/bjc/files/2026-08/3259250-Amounts-Generally-Billed.mp3)

## Your Right to a Good Faith Estimate

Under the law, health care providers need to give patients who don’t have insurance or who are not using insurance an estimate of the bill for medical items and services. To request an estimate, you may contact BJC HealthCare at  [314-747-8845](tel:3147478845) or toll free [844-747-8845](tel:8447478845).

[Good Faith Estimate](/sites/bjc/files/2026-02/Good-Faith-Estimate-02-2026.pdf) [Good Faith Estimate — Large Print](/sites/bjc/files/2026-03/Good-Faith-Estimate-Large-02-2026.pdf) [American Sign Language Video](https://youtu.be/_i_seESgSMk) [Audio File](/sites/bjc/files/2026-08/3259250-Good-Faith-Estimate.mp3)

## Understanding your costs

Understand the financial obligations and resources related to your medical care and services.

### Customized Cost Estimate

Health care pricing and patient billing can be complex, and at BJC HealthCare we strive to help our patients understand their financial liabilities before and after they receive care. BJC seeks to provide our patients with the highest quality of care available and accurate estimates of what their care may cost.

Working with our Patient Financial Representatives, you are able to receive a customized quote to help you understand your share of the cost. Call [314-747-8845](tel:3147478845) or toll free [844-747-8845](tel:8447478845). Or, you can submit the secure online Patient Cost Estimator form.

[**Get an Estimate**](/patients-visitors/billing-and-financial-assistance/cost-estimator)

## Your Rights and Protections Against Surprise Medical Bills

When you get emergency care or get treated by an out-of-network provider at an in-network hospital or ambulatory surgical center, you are protected from surprise billing or balance billing.

[**No Surprise Billing**](/sites/bjc/files/2025-02/No-Surprise-Billing-Act-notice.pdf)

## Regulatory Disclosures

### General Estimate Tool - Shoppable Services

In accordance with 2020 pricing transparency guidelines from the federal government, this tool allows you to view your costs for the most frequently used hospital services.

[**View patient cost estimator**](https://www.mypatientchart.org/mychart/guestestimates)

**Receive a customized quote**

We know that navigating health care costs can be confusing, so we also provide a personalized quote to help you understand your share of the cost. You can speak to one of our Patient Financial Representatives by calling [314-747-8845](tel:3147478845) or toll free [844-747-8845](tel:8447478845) or you can submit the secure online Patient Cost Estimator form to receive a customized quote.

[**Get an estimate**](/patients-visitors/billing-and-financial-assistance/cost-estimator)

### Regulatory Disclosure File

This regulatory disclosure file meets the CMS Hospital Price Transparency Regulation. Patients may request a [**customized cost estimate**](/patients-visitors/billing-and-financial-assistance/cost-estimator), as every patient’s needs are different and many factors impact your cost.
