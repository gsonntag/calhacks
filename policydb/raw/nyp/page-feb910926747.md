# Pay My Bill / Financial Aid

## Pay Online

If you have received a **Hospital bill** from NewYork-Presbyterian Hospital and would like to pay online, please visit the payment portal below.

[Pay Your Hospital Bill Online](/connect)

For Hospital billing questions, please contact the customer service center at [866-252-0101](tel:866-252-0101). You can also email [\[email protected\]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#4f3f293c272a233f0f21363f61203d28) or contact the Billing Office via message in [Connect](/connect).

## Self-Service Tools Available via Connect

### Payment Methods

If you have received a Hospital bill from NewYork-Presbyterian Hospital, you can save your preferred payment method in Connect and utilize the saved information whenever making a payment or for payment plans on auto-pay. For more info about Connect access, please go to [https://www.nyp.org/connect](/connect).

### Payment Plans

If you have received a **Hospital bill** from NewYork-Presbyterian Hospital and would like to pay in increments over a period of time, please utilize Connect to create a personalized payment plan that best suits your needs. For more info about Connect access, please go to [https://www.nyp.org/connect](/connect).

### Cost of Care Estimates/Shoppable Services

If you would like an estimate for the cost of scheduled or future care at NewYork- Presbyterian Hospital, please see our shoppable services tool at [Connect - Patient Estimates (myconnectnyc.org)](https://www.myconnectnyc.org/MyChart/GuestEstimates/AcceptDisclaimer?svcArea=WP-24BapuPHUXx56SH5YuLirgKA-3D-3D-24vaTJ3QX8tvqpaL-2FwIegrs87wE9xpkldu9ucwxFl9Xk0-3D "Connet Patient Estimates") to search through hundreds of listed prices online. For further assistance, please contact our Good Faith Estimates team via the [Estimate Patient Request Form](https://nyp.acquiadam.com/assets/share/asset/vod3c8hley).

### Insurance Coverage

At any point, patients can save their current insurance coverage in Connect to be used for future visits. For more info about Connect access, please go to [https://www.nyp.org/connect](/connect).

## Financial Aid

NewYork-Presbyterian has a long-standing policy to assist eligible patients who receive health care services at our hospital and are in need of financial aid, regardless of age, gender, race, national origin, socio-economic or immigrant status, sexual orientation or religious affiliation.

Various physicians and other service providers (providers) may provide care to patients at a Hospital facility. Hospital Ambulatory Care Network (ACN or clinic) providers are covered by Hospital's Charity Care Policy; other providers are not. Providers are described by category of service or department, and ACN (clinic), if applicable. Click below to access the list of providers. For more specific information about a particular provider, you may inquire with that provider. Access contact information for a specific provider from [find a doctor](https://doctors.nyp.org/).
