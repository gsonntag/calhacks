We’re here to help with billing matters related to your care at Zuckerberg San Francisco General. All patients must complete the hospital’s financial eligibility process. Then we can provide you with the best information about your bill. An eligibility worker will get your information to verify your insurance coverage or determine your eligibility for other programs if you do not have insurance.

Contact us at the number or email shown below, or send us a message through your [MyChart](https://mychart.sfdph.org/MyChart/Authentication/Login?) patient portal.

## Charge Description Master

### List of Billable Items

The Charge Description Master (CDM), or Chargemaster, is a listing of items that could be billed to a patient, payer, or health care provider. The most current Chargemaster can be found on the State of California Department of HealthCare Access and Information (HCAI) website.

[Go To Website](https://data.chhs.ca.gov/dataset/chargemasters)

[Average Medicare DRG Pricing](/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/ZSFG22-Average-Medicare-DRG-Pricing.pdf)

### Price Transparency

Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS), through its Price Transparency Rule, requires that hospitals make public standard charges. In compliance with the rule, we are making available the following:

A price estimator tool with shoppable services that allows consumers to estimate the amount they will be obligated to pay when receiving shoppable services.  
A Machine-Readable File with gross charges, discounted cash prices, payer-specific negotiated charges, and de-identified minimum and maximum negotiated charges.

### Shoppable Services

The prices for services are based on typical visits, and not intended to be representative of the services that may be required for every visit. The actual price a patient is responsible for may be higher or lower based on the actual services provided and a patient may receive separate estimates for professional services from SFGH Medical Group.

[SFDPH Patient Estimates](https://mychart.sfdph.org/MyChart/GuestEstimates/)

[CMS Regulations](https://www.cms.gov/priorities/key-initiatives/hospital-price-transparency/hospitals)

### Machine-Readable File of Standard Charges

This is a machine-readable file that contains a hospital’s gross charges and payer-specific negotiated charges, plus discounted cash prices, the de-identified minimum negotiated charge, and the de-identified maximum negotiated charge for all items and services provided by the hospital.

Call [628-206-8448](tel:+16282068448) for more information.

[CMS Regulations](https://www.cms.gov/priorities/key-initiatives/hospital-price-transparency/hospitals)

[Machine Readable File](https://sanfranciscogeneralhospitalfou.box.com/shared/static/bj0pj6iqcpc6bmb4yp7kobuz772j0jtl.csv)

### Payer’s Bill of Rights

The State of California established the Payer’s Bill of Rights which requires that each hospital:

*   Make a copy of its CDM (Chargemaster) available on its website.
*   Have available by request a list of the 20 most common procedures or services and five room and bed rates.

In compliance with California law, the Zuckerberg San Francisco General chargemaster is available above.
