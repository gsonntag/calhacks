## Eligibility Information

**To be eligible for the Charity Care Program:**

*   The patient’s family household income may not exceed 138% of the federal poverty level

**To be eligible for the Discount Payment Program:**

*   The patient is not eligible for the Charity Care Program
*   The patient’s household federal poverty level determines the amount of the discount to be applied

The Zuckerberg San Francisco General Hospital Charity Care and Discount Payment Program Policy is located on the State of California Department of Health Care Access and Information (HCAI) website.

[Go to Website](https://hcai.ca.gov/affordability/hospital-fair-billing-program/hospital-fair-pricing-policy-lookup/)

## How to Apply for Financial Assistance

SFDPH has a single application for both of the Charity Care and Discount Payment programs. Each program has different eligibility requirements, as described below. Patients must provide a completed application and all required verification.

*   Verification required by each program is requested within 30 days of starting an application.
*   You may mail an application to the following address:  
    Zuckerberg San Francisco General Hospital  
    Patient Financial Assistance Department  
    1001 Potrero Ave, Building 20,  
    San Francisco, CA 94110

## Hospital Bill Complaint Program

The Hospital Bill Complaint Program is a state program, which reviews hospital decisions about whether you qualify for help paying your hospital bill. If you believe you were wrongly denied financial assistance, you may file a complaint with the Hospital Bill Complaint Program. Go to HospitalBillComplaintProgram.hcai.ca.gov for more information and to file a complaint.
