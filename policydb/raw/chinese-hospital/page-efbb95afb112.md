# Help Paying Your Bill

Financial Assistance & Charity Care

Chinese Hospital is committed to helping patients who do not have the means to pay for emergency care and other medically necessary health care services through its financial assistance programs.

You can find details below about our financial assistance programs and how to apply.

**Financial Assistance Programs**  
If you have questions about these programs, contact us at:

**Patient Financial Services Department**  
Phone: [1-415-677-2314](tel:14156772314 " opens phone dialer")  
Email: [patientfinancialservices@chasf.org](mailto:patientfinancialservices@chasf.org " create new email")  
Office: 845 Jackson St, Lobby Floor, San Francisco, CA 94133  
Hours: Monday to Friday, 8:30 a.m. – 4:30 p.m.

**Financial Assistance:** Patient financial assistance in our Shoppable Services Program offers an intuitive way to help you estimate your out-of-pocket cost of care for common exams, procedures, tests, and services. The Shoppable Service Program can be found at [Price Transparency Page](https://apps.para-hcfs.com/PTT/FinalLinks/chineseCounty_V4.aspx " opens in a new window").

**Chinese Hospital Discount Policy:** Patients who do not have health insurance – or who need care that is not covered by an insurance plan – are considered self-pay patients and are eligible for a discount at Chinese Hospital. To find out more about our discount policy, call Patient Financial Services.

**Facing Financial Hardship?** We encourage you to apply to Medi-Cal and other government programs that may be of assistance.  If you do not qualify for those programs, or you have substantial financial liabilities despite having insurance, you may qualify for our financial assistance program at Chinese Hospital.

**Eligibility for Financial Assistance:** Financial assistance is based on your family size and income, even if you have health insurance. Families with an income at or below 400 percent of federal poverty level (FPL) guidelines are generally eligible for financial assistance from Chinese Hospital. Check here for details:

## HOW TO APPLY / RECEIVE A COPY

To apply for financial assistance, please download the application below and send it to: **Chinese Hospital Patient Financial Services Department, 845 Jackson St, Lobby Floor, San Francisco, CA 94133.**

To receive a free copy of the FAP and FAP application form by mail, please contact our Patient Financial Services Department. A representative will obtain your mailing address and mail the application form to you.

For assistance with applying for FAP, a representative from Patient Financial Services will help you with the form. A completed application can be mailed to Patient Financial Services, 845 Jackson St, San Francisco, CA 94133. For faster service, you may email to [patientfinancialservices@chasf.org](mailto:patientfinancialservices@chasf.org " create new email").

Note: Your completed application for financial assistance should be submitted after services are provided and is not a substitute for insurance or a guarantee of future services.

## Questions

## Complaint

The California Department of Health Care Access and Information (HCAI) Hospital Bill Complaint Program investigates patient complaints and can assess administrative penalties against hospitals for violations of the Hospital Fair Pricing Act. If you believe you were wrongly denied financial assistance, [you may file an online complaint to HCAI](https://hcai.ca.gov/affordability/hospital-fair-billing-program/ " opens in a new window").
