## Medical Financial Assistance (MFA)

The MFA program provides financial assistance to qualified patients who are having difficulty paying their Kaiser Permanente medical or pharmacy bills.

**Note**: This award is not transferrable to other health care services outside of Kaiser Permanente.

### MFA eligibility and guidelines

![clipboard](/content/dam/kporg/final/graphics/icons/illustrative/clipboard-pencil-illustrative.svg)

#### Think you might be eligible?

The level of financial assistance is based on two factors:

1. A patient whose yearly household income is less than or equal to 300% or 400% of the [Federal Poverty Guidelines (“FPG”)](https://aspe.hhs.gov/topics/poverty-economic-mobility/poverty-guidelines "Opens in a new window, external")﻿ depending on your region of care.

2\. If you’re experiencing high medical expenses as compared to your income, regardless of household income.

**Want to learn if you’re eligible? Use our eligibility calculator below**

![family](/content/dam/kporg/final/graphics/icons/illustrative/family-child-baby-illustrative.svg)

#### Want to apply for financial assistance?

Apply for financial assistance if you think you qualify. For ease, apply online. Additionally, you may apply using a paper application to mail, fax, or drop-off.

Once a completed application is received, you’ll hear back from us within 30 days. If it is not approved, we will provide a reason for the denial and instructions on how to appeal the decision if you disagree.

[I want to apply](#mfa-ready-to-apply)

For more information about Medical Financial Assistance, read our [frequently asked questions.](/help-paying-your-bill/medical-financial-assistance)

### Community Health Coverage Program (CHCP)

Kaiser Permanente’s Charitable Health Coverage program provides coverage and subsidies to qualified applicants that help pay for monthly premiums and most out-of-pocket medical costs at Kaiser Permanente facilities.  
[Learn about the CHCP program](https://charitablehealth.kaiserpermanente.org/)  
  

### Kaiser Permanente Community Support Hub

If you need help with essentials like food, housing, or paying for internet or other utilities, visit **kp.org/socialhealth**.
