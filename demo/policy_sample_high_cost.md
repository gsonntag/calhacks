Eastshore Medical Center - Financial Assistance Policy (Charity Care)

Section 3. Eligibility
Uninsured patients whose family income is at or below 400% of the Federal Poverty Level (FPL) qualify for full charity care: 100% of the patient's account balance is written off.
Insured patients whose family income is at or below 400% of the FPL qualify for full charity care (100% of the patient balance written off) if their out-of-pocket medical expenses in the prior 12 months exceed 10% of their family income (High Medical Cost patients).

Section 5. Collections
The hospital will not send an account to collections while a financial assistance application is pending.
