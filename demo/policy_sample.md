Bayview Community Hospital - Financial Assistance Policy (Charity Care and Discount Payment)

Section 4. Eligibility
Patients whose family income is at or below 200% of the Federal Poverty Level (FPL) qualify for full charity care: 100% of the patient's account balance is written off.
Patients whose family income is above 200% and at or below 400% of the FPL qualify for discounted payment: the patient's balance is reduced by 75%.

Section 5. Applying
Patients may apply at any time before or after billing, including after an account has been referred for collection. Proof of income may include recent pay stubs or bank statements covering the last 3 months.

Section 6. Collections
The hospital will not send an account to collections while a financial assistance application is pending. Extended payment plans for eligible patients are interest-free.
